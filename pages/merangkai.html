a look into Indonesia

The Problem

How might we preserve Indonesian cultural heritage while making it feel engaging, accessible, and relevant in a digital world?

Indonesian cultural heritage is rich with stories, symbols, and artistic traditions, yet much of it is experienced through static documentation rather than interactive experiences.

As digital media becomes an increasingly important way for people to discover and learn, traditional forms such as Ondel-ondel, Barong, and batik risk feeling distant from younger, digitally native audiences.

The Research

How to gain
recognition

I explored Indonesian cultural traditions that carry strong visual languages and cultural narratives, focusing on how they could be translated into interactive digital experiences.

Rather than simply digitizing these traditions, I looked at how their visual patterns, movement, symbolism, and narratives could become interactive systems.

This led to a key insight:

Digital preservation does not have to mean simply archiving culture. It can also mean creating new ways to experience it.

The Opportunity

Generative art and computational design offered a natural bridge between traditional craftsmanship and modern technology.

This created an opportunity to:

  • Make Indonesian heritage more approachable to digital audiences.
  • Preserve cultural stories through new forms of digital media.
  • Turn traditional visual systems into interactive experiences.
  • Demonstrate how technology can reinterpret culture without losing its identity.

The project became an exploration of how tradition and technology can coexist rather than compete.

The Solution

Merangkai: Reimagining Indonesian heritage through interaction

Merangkai transforms Indonesian cultural heritage into interactive digital experiences through generative art and computational design. Users can explore Ondel-ondel, Barong, and batik through animated narratives, interactive visuals, and generative patterns, making traditional stories more engaging and accessible to digital audiences.
