01 · Problem & research

Closest isn't the same as convenient

When people travel and want to stop for food, map tools rank by distance from a point. But a place can be 0.3 miles away and still cost 15 minutes off the route.

Today's workflow

Plan route→Search restaurants→Compare locations→Check directions→Estimate detour

Five steps, two tools, and the key number (added time) is guesswork.

The insight

Useful info isn't how far a restaurant is. It's how much stopping there changes the journey.
